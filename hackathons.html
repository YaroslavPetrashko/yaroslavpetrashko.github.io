ShellHacks 2024 (Florida's Largest Hackathon) — LocalPulse

September 2024 — Winner (Waymo)

Built LocalPulse, a comprehensive community intelligence app for the City of Coral Gables that puts the power of neighborhood insights in your hands. The platform combines crime analysis, real estate analytics, small business details, RAG chatbot, and city historical trends with real-time data including police reports, traffic, weather, and live camera feeds with object detection.

Following the win, our team was personally invited by the Mayor of Coral Gables to visit the City's GIS & IoT Lab for a discussion on improving their urban infrastructure. We met with city staff and interns and exchanged ideas around open data resources and improving citizen experience tools.

Python · LangChain · GCP · Gemini API · PostgreSQL · Streamlit · Docker

HackBeanpot 2025 (Northeastern University) — Memora

February 2025 — Winner (Social Impact)

Built Memora, a personalized memory support app for individuals experiencing early to moderate dementia. The app uses personal family photographs to stimulate memory recall through contextual quizzes, with an intuitive interface that guides users through their memories. Families can upload and tag photos by people, places, and events, generating personalized cue-based exercises.

React · JavaScript · Supabase · Express.js

HackHarvard 2025 (Harvard University) — FamiliAR

October 2025

Built FamiliAR, an immersive spatial app for Apple Vision Pro designed to help Alzheimer's patients reconnect with loved ones through interactive, memory-based experiences. Relatives upload photos through a web portal, where images transform into vivid spatial memories. Inside Vision Pro, patients experience lifelike visuals accompanied by familiar voices generated via ElevenLabs voice cloning, with personalized quiz-like interactions to assess recall patterns.

Apple Vision Pro SDK · RealityKit · SwiftUI · ElevenLabs API · Supabase · React · Tailwind

Miami Hack Week 2024 — NeoSapiens

April 2024 — Winner (Collegiate Level)

Built NeoSapiens, a system that automates the creation of domain-expert AI agents to solve complex problems using hierarchical swarm intelligence. The platform breaks down problems into smaller steps, with each agent addressing tasks within its expertise, then aggregates outputs into structured solutions. Agents can interact with developer tools like terminals and browsers to autonomously complete tasks.

Python · Swarms · Claude API
