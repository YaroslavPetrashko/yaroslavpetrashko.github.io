HackMIT 2024 — NeuralScope

October 2024

Developed a real-time neural network visualization tool that lets users interactively explore how deep learning models process information layer by layer. Users can upload images and watch activations propagate through the network in an intuitive 3D interface.

The project was built with Three.js for visualization and PyTorch for the backend inference engine. Presented to a panel of judges from leading AI research labs.
